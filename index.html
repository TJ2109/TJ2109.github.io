I always want to know how a car works and how each part is designed to do its job efficiently. That curiosity is why I enjoyed my time with the Norton team at TVS and the plant and shop-floor visits.

Inspired by nature

Real-Time ADAS Visual Horn Indication System

TVS Motor Company · Embedded AI · Safety

The problem: riders who are distracted or hearing-impaired can miss what is happening around them. This sound-based system prompts them visually when a horn sounds nearby, validated on the TVS Jupiter 125.

  • Direction-of-arrival detection from MEMS I2S microphones using GCC-PHAT with a deep learning model, designed for low latency
  • Model trained on more than 1,000 real-time audio samples
  • Hardware bring-up, JTAG/GDB debugging and integration testing in the Power Electronics Lab (ESP32, Raspberry Pi)
  • Milestones tracked in Jira across the R&D and Data/AI teams; I led a two-intern shift team

Audio to Visual Transcript

Generative AI · NLP · Storytelling

The problem: people who cannot hear, who struggle to concentrate on audio, or who are students learning from it, have little to hold onto. This turns audio, including your own stories, into visuals and video so it is easier to understand. Speech is transcribed, analysed and converted into scene-by-scene prompts for image generation.

  • Whisper transcription, then sentiment, entity, keyword and emotion analysis
  • Scores each segment to pick the key story moments, with selectable styles (cinematic, anime, watercolour)
  • Generated frames are assembled into an .mp4 with OpenCV
